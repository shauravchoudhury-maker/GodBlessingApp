How a blessing works
1You write.

Pick your faith and your language, and say what you are carrying. No account, no email. You get a private link.

2A guide who has lived it reads.

Not whoever is free — someone matched on what they have been through. Grief goes to someone who has grieved.

3A verse comes back, and a few words of their own.

Usually within a day or two. If one blessing is not enough, you can ask to keep talking — only you can open that door.

If what you write suggests you are in danger, you are shown help in your own country first, and your request only goes to a guide we have checked most carefully.

What a guide can never do

Not "should not". These are refused by the system itself, whatever anyone intends.

  • Write to you more than once — unless you ask.
  • Edit or delete what they sent you.
  • Give you contact details, a link, or a way to reach them elsewhere.
  • Ask you for money, or anything at all.
  • Tell you to stop medication or skip a doctor.
  • Read anyone's request but the ones matched to them.
  • Sign as "Dr." or "Therapist" — we cannot verify it, so we do not allow it.
  • Know whether you ever gave anything.

Every guide is approved by a person, and the ones who hold longer conversations are identity-checked and vouched for by two others. On each guide's card we show what we have checked and, with equal weight, what we have not.

If you want to give

Nobody who asks pays. Nobody who answers pays. If you would like to give, your gift goes to a charity of your faith — never to a guide, and never tied to a blessing.
