Write it, and let it go.

Whatever is weighing on you — a regret, a resentment, something you have not said out loud. Write it here, in your own words. Then release it, and receive a word on mercy from your own tradition.

This never leaves your screen. Nothing you write here is sent anywhere or saved anywhere. There is no account, no guide, no record. We could not read it if we wanted to.
  • No button on this page sends anything to us.
  • Spell-check is switched off on the box, so your browser does not send the words to anyone either.
  • When you press Let it go, the words are cleared from this page and from memory.

Some of what you have written suggests you may be in real danger right now. Nothing here has been sent to anyone, and it will not be. But a verse is not enough for this. Please talk to someone trained, today.

It is gone.

Nothing was kept. Not here, not anywhere.

If you would rather a person wrote back to you about this — someone from your tradition who has carried something too — the Blessing Circle is here. That is a different door, and it is always open.

This is a private practice, not a confession and not a sacrament, and nobody at EverVerse absolves anyone. If your tradition has its own way of bringing these things to God — reconciliation, teshuvah, tawbah — this does not replace it. It is a place to set something down. If you are unwell, please also speak to a doctor.
