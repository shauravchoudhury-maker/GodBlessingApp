EverVerse — the Blessing Circle campaign
========================================

The code is in campaign.js. This is the part that is not code: what to do
first, where, and what would make it go wrong.


THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS MOST
-------------------------------
There are zero approved guides today.

If we market the Circle to people in pain before there are guides, somebody
writes down the worst thing in their life, presses send, and nothing comes
back. That is worse than never having offered. It is also the fastest way
to lose the only asset this platform has, which is being trusted — and it
is unrecoverable, because the person it happened to will never come back
and will tell people.

So: SUPPLY FIRST. Recruit guides for six to eight weeks. Only then invite
people to ask.

campaign.js enforces this rather than trusting the plan. GUIDE_COUNTS is a
hand-edited constant, and until a faith reaches three approved guides the
daily posts say "we need guides", not "come and ask". Switching it is a
decision someone makes on purpose after looking at who those guides are —
not something that happens because a query returned a number.


WHERE THE FIRST GUIDES COME FROM
--------------------------------
Not ads. In order of how well they will work:

1. THE AUDIENCE WE ALREADY HAVE. Whoever opens the daily verse is already
   religious, already here, and has never been asked. This is the single
   best source and it costs nothing. The daily post CTA does this.

2. THE ETSY BUYERS. Someone who bought a scripture print for their mother's
   funeral is exactly the person who should be answering requests about
   losing a parent. A card in the digital download, one line, no pressure.

3. FAITH COMMUNITIES, ONE AT A TIME. A letter to a parish, a mosque, a
   gurdwara, a temple, a synagogue. Not a mail merge — a real letter naming
   the specific thing we want: two or three people who have been through
   something and would answer one message a week. This is slow and it is
   the only route that produces STEWARDS, because the institution can then
   confirm the affiliation.

4. HOSPICE AND CHAPLAINCY VOLUNTEER NETWORKS. People already doing this
   work in person, already background-checked by someone.

5. Reddit and Facebook faith groups. Read the self-promotion rules first
   and follow them; getting banned from r/Christianity would cost more than
   it gains.

Do NOT buy ads for guide recruitment. The people who respond to a paid ad
for "become a spiritual guide" are, on average, not the people we want, and
the ones we do want are unreachable that way.


THE RATIO
---------
Per campaign.js monthPlan(): out of 28 days —

  20  daily verse posts, with one quiet campaign line appended
   4  trust posts (weekly)
   4  guide recruitment posts (weekly), rotating across the seven faiths

The daily verse is the traffic. It is what gets shared, and a verse post
that turns into an advert stops being shared, which kills the engine. Hence
one short line, rotating, and NEVER on a post about grief or peace — a
verse someone opens while hurting is not an acquisition channel. That
suppression is in the code, not left to judgement.


THE POSITIONING
---------------
Every competitor in "spiritual wellness" advertises what it can do FOR you.
Almost nobody advertises what it structurally cannot do TO you.

That is the gap, it is defensible, and it is the only claim we can keep
under scrutiny. So the trust posts are the campaign's spine:

  · A guide can write to you exactly once. Not policy — the system refuses
    a second message.
  · A guide cannot give you contact details, a link, or a way to reach them
    anywhere else.
  · Nobody can ask you for money. There is no paid tier.
  · A guide cannot read anyone's request but yours. There is no queue to
    browse. We used to allow it. We closed it.
  · A guide cannot sign as "Dr." or "Therapist", even if they are one.
  · If a request reads as coming from someone under 18, we do not pass it
    on. There is no override.
  · If someone is in real danger, it goes to an identity-checked,
    background-checked guide — or we say we cannot take it and point at 988.

Every one of those is true today and provable by reading the code.

A note on "biggest". It is the wrong goal to chase first and a dangerous
one to claim — campaign.js refuses the word, along with "#1", "the best"
and "most trusted", because we cannot prove any of them. The way to end up
biggest in this category is to be the one that never has the scandal.
Scale is the reward for being trusted, not the route to it.


WHAT WE WILL NEVER DO
---------------------
Enforced by checkCampaignCopy() and tested. In summary:

  · No health or outcome claims. No "cure", "heal your", "fix your".
  · Never positioned against medical care or therapy.
  · No clinical targeting. Never buy or hashtag "depression", "anxiety
    disorder", "PTSD", "addict".
  · NEVER target crisis terms — "suicidal", "want to die", "self-harm".
    Advertising at people in crisis is ambulance-chasing, and doing it
    while claiming to be the safe option would be indefensible.
  · No life-event targeting on Meta or Google. The platforms will happily
    let us target "recently bereaved". We do not.
  · No testimonials. A recipient's words are theirs. We never quote what
    somebody wrote to us, in any form, however moving, however anonymised.
  · No manufactured urgency, no invented social proof, no unprovable
    superlatives, no implied paid tier.

The check runs on OUR copy, never on the scripture we quote — Gita 9:34
says "Fix your mind on me" and Matthew 28:6 says "just as he said", and the
right response to a filter flagging those is obviously not to edit
scripture. Use checkCaption(caption, verse), which strips the verse first.


HOW WE WILL KNOW IT IS WORKING
------------------------------
Not by traffic. The numbers that matter, in order:

  1. Approved guides per faith. Below three, that faith is not open.
  2. Median time to a reply. If it goes past three days, stop all
     requester-facing marketing until it comes down.
  3. Requests that never got an answer. This should be zero. If it is not
     zero, nothing else on this list matters.
  4. Reports per hundred blessings, and how many were upheld.
  5. Only then: requests received.

If 2 or 3 go wrong, the correct response is to turn the campaign OFF, not
to recruit faster.


FIRST FOUR WEEKS, CONCRETELY
----------------------------
Week 1  Publish BLESSING_RULES.txt. Nothing below works until the rules are
        live — right now every write returns 403.
        Approve yourself as admin. Recruit posts begin on the daily feed.
Week 2  Letters to five institutions, one per faith you have a contact in.
        Etsy download card goes out with new orders.
Week 3  First applications arrive. Ring the organisations. Approve slowly —
        the first hundred guides set the culture permanently.
Week 4  First trust post. Review: how many guides per faith?
        Three in any faith → set GUIDE_COUNTS and let that faith open.
        Fewer → keep recruiting and say nothing else. That is not a delay,
        it is the product working.
