EverVerse — launching the Blessing Circle
=========================================
Written 11 September 2026. The Circle is the core of the platform; the
Library, the daily verse and the social feed exist to bring people to it
and to bring guides to it.

WHAT "GLOBAL IN OCTOBER" HONESTLY MEANS
---------------------------------------
The door is open to the world from the day PR #40 merges: anyone, anywhere,
in any of the 24 languages, can ask. That part is global on day one.

PROMOTION is not. A language is promoted only once a native speaker has
read its crisis and under-18 patterns (safety-lang.js, every `checked` is
null today). A country is promoted only once its helpline numbers have been
verified against the provider's own site (world.js, every `checked` is null
today). A faith opens for requests only once three approved guides can
answer in it (campaign.js GUIDE_COUNTS, all zero today).

So October is: OPEN TO EVERYONE, PROMOTED WHERE IT IS SAFE. That is not a
smaller launch. It is the only version that survives contact with the
first person in Delhi who is handed a helpline that does not connect.


THE GATES, IN ORDER

Each gate is a thing that must be TRUE, not a date. Dates are targets.

THE ANNOUNCEMENT — recruit phase only
-------------------------------------
Generated from campaign.js and checked against its NEVER list. Rotate
across the seven faiths; one recruitment post a week alongside the daily
verse, and one trust post a week. Do not post more often than that — a
verse feed that turns into an advert stops being shared.

  [Lived, Christianity]
  Somebody wrote to us tonight about losing their mother. What they need
  is not a professional. It is someone who has stood where they are
  standing.

  If you have buried a parent, and you read the Bible, the Blessing
  Circle needs you.

  No charge, ever, to anyone — for the person asking or for you.
  eververse.org/guides.html

  [Small ask, any faith]
  It is one message. Not a rota, not a commitment, not a call — one verse
  and a few words of your own, when you have the time.

  That is the whole ask. You can answer one and never come back.
  eververse.org/guides.html

  [Language, Islam / Hinduism / Sikhism]
  There are people who can only pray properly in the language they
  learned it in.

  If you can write in Urdu, Punjabi, Gujarati, Tamil, Spanish or Arabic,
  you can answer someone nobody else can reach.
  eververse.org/guides.html

  [Hard bar, any faith]
  We turn down more applications than we accept, and we check the ones
  we keep.

  If that sounds like the kind of place you would want answering your
  own mother — apply.
  eververse.org/guides.html

  [Trust post — the one to pin]
  Every blessing on EverVerse is written by a person. Not generated. Not
  suggested. A guide who has been where you are reads what you wrote and
  writes back in their own words.

  As more of life gets answered by machines, that is the promise we
  built the platform around. It is slower than a chatbot. It is also the
  point.
  eververse.org

Never in any post: "AI-powered", "instant", "#1", "biggest", "cure",
"therapy", any crisis term as a hashtag, or any invitation to ask for a
blessing before Gate 4. campaign.js refuses all of these if you run the
copy through checkCampaignCopy().


THE LETTER TO AN INSTITUTION
----------------------------
Send to a named person — a chaplain, a parish administrator, a mosque
committee member, a gurdwara secretary, a hospice volunteer coordinator.
Not a mail merge. Name the specific thing you want.

  Dear [name],

  I run EverVerse, a small platform where a person who is struggling can
  write a few lines — about a bereavement, an illness, a marriage, a
  faith that has gone quiet — and have someone from their own tradition
  read it and write back with a verse and a few words of their own. There
  is no charge to anyone and no account required. Every reply is written
  by a person; we do not use AI to write them and never will.

  I am writing because the people who are best at this are not
  professionals. They are people who have been through the same thing.
  I am looking for two or three members of [congregation / your
  volunteer team] who have carried a loss, an illness or a hard season
  and would be willing to answer one message a week from someone going
  through it now. That is the entire commitment: one message, in their
  own time, from their own home.

  What we do to keep everyone safe, because you will want to know:
  every guide is approved by hand after a conversation with me; a guide
  can write to a person only once unless that person asks to continue;
  the system itself refuses contact details, links, requests for money
  and any advice about medication; requests that suggest someone is in
  danger go only to guides we have identity-checked and background-
  checked, alongside the local crisis line; and no request can ever be
  browsed or searched. The full list is on the site.

  If you were willing to put your name to two or three people, we would
  confirm that with you directly and show it on their profile as
  "confirmed with [institution]" — which is worth more to a frightened
  person than anything we could say about ourselves.

  I would be glad to come and explain it in person, or on a call, at
  whatever time suits you.

  With thanks,
  [name]
  eververse.org · [phone]

Do not mention the giving model in the first letter. That conversation
comes after they have seen their people answer real requests, and it
should come from them asking.


THE GIVING MODEL
----------------
EverVerse takes NOTHING from a gift. All of it, less the card fee (which
the giver can cover), goes to a charity of the giver's faith. Separately
and optionally, the giver may add a tip to EverVerse — never taken from the
gift, never preselected, its purpose listed. Decided 11 September 2026,
after 10% and 1% were both considered: zero is the strongest true sentence
a giving page can carry, it is what raises more (GoFundMe's model since
2017), and a voluntary tip is not a cut of a donation, which lifts most of
the fundraising-platform registration burden. No percentage of gifts was
going to fund year one anyway — that is the Etsy business, partner
institutions covering their own guides' checks, and grants once there is a
501(c)(3). Revisit once real gift volume exists.


THE LIBRARY, AND WHY IT IS FREE
-------------------------------
library.html gives away, for personal use: the daily verses to browse and
save, the 23 written devotionals, greeting cards for 13 occasions made from
any verse, and the five audiobook manuscripts to read or print.

Digital is free; print is Etsy. That is a deliberate split, not a leak.
Free digital brings people to the door and gives them a reason to come
back before they ever need the Circle. The Etsy prints are a different
product for a different moment — a gift, a wall — and the two have never
competed. If that changes, the license line on library.html is where to
change it.
