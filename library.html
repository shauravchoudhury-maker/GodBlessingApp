Devotionals

Short written reflections, two or three minutes each, every one tied to a verse. Written by people, for reading slowly.

Greeting cards

Pick an occasion and a verse, and take the card away as an image to send or print. Diwali, Eid, Christmas, a birthday, a sympathy card — from any tradition.

Audiobook manuscripts

The written text of each EverVerse audiobook — verse, plain-language meaning and reflection, chapter by chapter. Read on screen, or print and keep by the bed.

Write it, and let it go

A regret, a resentment, something you have not said out loud. Write it, release it, and receive a word on mercy from your own tradition. It never leaves your screen — nothing is sent, nothing is saved, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

Not a confession and not a sacrament — nobody absolves anyone here. A place to set something down.
